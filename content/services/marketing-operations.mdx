This is the least interesting page on the site to read and the most consequential one to get right. Lead routing, deduplication, field mapping, and stack consolidation do not produce a headline metric of their own — they determine whether any of the other twenty-two programs on this site can be measured at all. A campaign judged against a lead count that includes duplicates, or a form that silently drops into the wrong inbox, is not measuring marketing performance. It is measuring how well the plumbing happened to work that month.

We do not apologize for how unglamorous this work is. It is the precondition for everything else, and it gets built or fixed first for exactly that reason.

## How the work differs

## How it is measured

This page does not have its own conversion metric — its success is measured in whether every other program's numbers can be trusted. The test is simple to state and hard to fake: pull the same lead from the ad platform, the website, and the CRM, and confirm it is recognized as one person with one consistent source, not three disconnected records. Where that test fails, whatever [attribution](/services/marketing-attribution) is being reported elsewhere on the site is standing on an unstable foundation, regardless of how sophisticated the reporting on top of it looks.

What this cannot claim credit for: a specific campaign's performance. This work does not generate demand; it determines whether demand that already exists gets counted correctly. Crediting it with a lift in bookings would be attributing the plumbing with performance that belongs to whichever program actually produced the lead.

## What we need from you

Administrative access to the CRM, the marketing automation platform, and however forms and calls currently get routed — this is the one page where read-only access is not enough, since the fixes happen inside these systems directly. A walkthrough from whoever currently handles lead routing manually, if anyone does, since undocumented manual workarounds are often where the real routing logic actually lives. And authority to change routing rules and field structures, since half-measures agreed to in a meeting but never implemented are the most common reason this work stalls.

## Who this is for

Built for operators running marketing across more than one location or channel, where leads are getting lost, duplicated, or misrouted somewhere between the ad click and the sales team — [multi-location healthcare groups](/industries/healthcare-marketing) hit this constantly, since intake often spans a call center, a web form, and a front-desk system that were never connected. It sits among the [bottom-of-funnel](/services/bottom-of-funnel) programs as the one whose job is making the other six trustworthy. This is the closest pairing on the site to [marketing attribution](/services/marketing-attribution) itself: attribution tells you what happened, and this work determines whether what gets recorded is even accurate in the first place.

It combines directly with [conversion rate optimization](/services/conversion-rate-optimization), since post-form intake speed depends entirely on routing working correctly, and with [email and lifecycle marketing](/services/email-marketing), which needs clean, deduplicated contact records to function at all.
