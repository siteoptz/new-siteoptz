A call placed from a Google Business Profile, a direction request tapped, an hours check before someone walks in — every one of those actions happens entirely inside Google's own interface. None of it generates a page view, a session, or a form submission on the business's own site, so a report built purely from site analytics misses this volume as a structural gap in the measurement, regardless of how well any individual tag is configured.

This is also where branded and local demand gets decided. Someone checking a map pack listing has usually already narrowed the choice to a handful of nearby options; the work here is winning that specific moment, which is a different job from the non-brand SEO work that got the business considered in the first place.

## How it is measured

A call placed from a Business Profile, a direction request tapped, or an hours check happens entirely inside Google's own interface and never touches a business's site analytics — which is why a report built only from site data measures a fraction of this channel, not the channel itself. What gets connected directly: calls through a dedicated tracking number, direction requests, and website clicks, all reported at the location level through Google's own profile insights. What stays estimated: whether a specific profile view eventually became a specific booked customer, resolved with the same identity-matching approach used across the [attribution layer](/services/marketing-attribution) and flagged with the same confidence scoring.

What this channel cannot claim credit for: a booking from someone who had not yet decided to consider the business at all — that demand is earned through non-brand SEO, and crediting a branded-query conversion to local capture work would double-count against it.

## What we need from you

Ownership-level access to every location's Business Profile, since claiming an unclaimed listing, editing verified details, and responding to reviews all require it directly rather than a corporate view with no location-level control. A response protocol for the review categories that need your team's input before anything posts — a serious complaint, a legal threat, a factual dispute you want to correct personally. And a fast point of contact for a profile suspension, since reinstatement timelines are already set by Google and any delay on your side adds directly to how long a location stays off the map pack.
