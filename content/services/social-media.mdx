Every vendor selling organic social management eventually faces the same problem: the channel rarely produces a directly trackable inquiry of its own, and a client paying a monthly retainer wants to see it produce something. The common response in this category is to quietly borrow credit from paid social - reporting a conversion that a paid ad actually drove as evidence that the organic posting is working. It is a comfortable lie for everyone involved right up until someone compares the two channels' numbers side by side and finds them suspiciously identical.

The honest position is less flattering and more useful. Organic social's real effect shows up as branded search lift and as an assisted touch somewhere in a longer path to a booking, not as a channel with its own clean conversion count. Reporting it that way is less exciting than a fabricated revenue number, and it is the only version that survives being checked against what paid social is separately reporting.

## How it is measured

Branded search volume is tracked before, during, and after a sustained posting cadence, watching for a lift that correlates with active periods and fades during quiet ones - reported plainly as a correlation, with the same hedge attached every time it is cited. Separately, any booked outcome where an organic social touch shows up earlier in the recorded path gets flagged as an assisted conversion inside the same [attribution layer](/services/marketing-attribution) used across every other channel, credited specifically as contributing influence alongside whatever touch actually closed it.

What this channel cannot claim credit for: a conversion actually produced by a paid social ad, a search ad, or any other channel with its own direct measurement, even where an organic post also appeared somewhere in that same customer's history. Follower count and engagement rate are tracked strictly as operational health metrics for the program itself.

## What we need from you

Someone on your team available to weigh in quickly when a comment or message needs specific business knowledge to answer correctly, since a wrong answer posted publicly is harder to undo than a short delay waiting for the right one. Access to whatever analytics tool tracks branded search volume, so the lift comparison has a real baseline to work from. And real events, announcements, and photography from the business itself, since a calendar built entirely from generic content ideas produces exactly the generic-feeling presence this approach is meant to avoid.
