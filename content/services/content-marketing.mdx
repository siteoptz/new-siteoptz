A last-click report values content close to zero, and that is a measurement failure rather than an honest reflection of what the content did. A prospect who read three articles over two months before finally filling out a form gets recorded as a conversion from whatever channel that final form submission happened to carry - the three articles that actually moved the decision along the way are invisible to a tool that only sees the last touch.

The fix is recording, inside the CRM where deals actually get tracked, which specific pieces a prospect engaged with on the way to becoming an opportunity. That record only becomes useful once enough deals have closed to see a pattern, which means a single month's report will usually understate a piece written recently - its real contribution shows up later, once the deals it influenced actually close.

## How it is measured

The number that matters is how often a piece appears in the recorded content path of a deal that actually closed. That path is built from CRM records: a session tells you someone visited a page, while a closed-deal record tells you that visit was part of something that turned into revenue. That data flows into the [attribution layer](/services/marketing-attribution) on the same schedule as every other touchpoint on the site.

What this channel cannot claim credit for: a deal that closed without any recorded content engagement in its path, and a piece's traffic volume alone, independent of whether that traffic ever shows up in a closed-deal record.

## What we need from you

A standing line to your sales and support teams, since the actual questions worth writing about surface directly in their calls and tickets. Subject-matter review turnaround fast enough that a draft does not sit waiting while a real sales question goes unanswered in print. And access to the CRM fields where content engagement gets logged against a deal, so the path can actually be reconstructed once that deal closes.

## Who this is for

Built for operators with a sales cycle long enough that a buyer reads, compares, and returns more than once before deciding - [professional services firms](/industries/professional-services-marketing) fit this closely, since a firm being evaluated against two or three competitors over several weeks needs content answering the specific comparison questions that come up in that exact window. Among the [middle-of-funnel](/services/middle-of-funnel) programs, it is distributed in part through [organic social](/services/social-media), and shares much of its raw material with [SEO](/services/seo), since the same well-researched article often serves both the ranking work and the deal-path work at once.

For an operator also running [branded search](/services/branded-search-ppc), the strongest evaluation-stage pieces frequently end up linked directly from that campaign's ad copy and landing pages, giving a brand click something substantive to land on beyond the homepage.
