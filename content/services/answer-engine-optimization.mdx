Winning the featured snippet is supposed to be the win. Often it is also the moment traffic to that page drops, because the answer the snippet promises is now sitting directly in the search results, and a real share of searchers who would have clicked through to read it get what they needed without ever leaving the results page. A standard analytics report sees only the traffic side of that trade and reports a loss on a page that just became the source of an answer shown to more people than ever.

Presence and traffic diverge here in a way most channels never have to confront, and treating the two as if they move together is exactly how a genuine win gets reported as a regression. The work in this discipline runs on tracking presence as its own number and being honest about which situations actually call for chasing it.

## When zero-click visibility is still worth having

A snippet win that costs clicks is not automatically a loss. Brand visibility from owning the answer to a common question reaches people who never click through to any result at all, competitors included, and ceding that space simply hands the same visibility to a competitor's page, with the lost traffic staying gone either way. Optimizing for voice means accepting that voice queries carry no fallback list a searcher can scroll past, so being the one answer read aloud is the entire opportunity, with no consolation traffic available either way. Where a page's snippet-driven visibility supports a business's presence at a stage before someone is ready to click anything at all, presence itself is the return being measured, and it is reported as such.

## How it is measured

The number tracked is presence rate: how often, across a fixed set of target queries checked on a schedule, the business owns the snippet, the People Also Ask entry, or the voice result. Where a snippet does send a click, that click gets matched to whatever it eventually produced inside the [attribution layer](/services/marketing-attribution), and reported alongside the presence number as a second, complementary data point.

What this channel cannot claim credit for: general non-brand ranking position or traffic on queries that never surface a snippet at all, which is judged as [SEO](/services/seo), or a mention inside a generated AI assistant answer, which is a different mechanism tracked as generative engine optimization.

## What we need from you

A list of the specific questions your buyers actually type or ask a voice assistant before choosing a vendor - usually long-tail, question-form queries - since query selection starts from real language rather than a guess at what sounds searchable. Whoever maintains the site's CMS, for the structural changes - headers, lists, tables - that make a page extractable. And an accurate read on how much value brand visibility itself carries for the business, since that judgment shapes which snippet wins are worth pursuing even where click-through is expected to fall.
